How an engagement runs

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We sit down with the people who actually touch the records. We map what exists, where it lives, and what "analysis-ready" needs to mean for the questions you want to ask.

  2. 02

    On-site conversion

    We work in your building, on your terms. Paper-era records become structured, de-identified data without a single identifiable record leaving the premises.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    You get an analysis-ready dataset and the documentation to trust it — plus the re-identification key, which stays with you and only you.
