Two founders. One hard boundary.

Cinnabar is Daniel and Paul — a CEO and a CTO building AI-native data and workflow products around a single promise: sensitive data stays where it belongs. That promise is the whole point, so we built the company small enough to keep it.

  • Daniel CEO

    Daniel leads product and how the work gets scoped — deciding what we build, who it is for, and where the hard data boundaries sit before a single record is touched.

  • Paul CTO

    Paul leads the engineering — the AI-native pipeline that turns sensitive records into analysis-ready data, and the technical controls that keep identifiable data where it belongs.

Why two people is a feature, not a bug

No handoffs, no telephone game

The person who scopes your engagement is the person who builds it. Requirements don't get lost passing through a sales team, a project manager, and three layers of engineers — because those layers don't exist.

The founders own the guarantees

When we put our data commitments in writing, it's the two people who designed the architecture standing behind them. There's nobody to defer to and nobody to blame. That's the accountability you want when the data is this sensitive.

Small on purpose, not by accident

A larger vendor spreads sensitive work across more hands and more systems. We keep the surface area small — fewer people who touch your records, fewer places your data can go. Careful is easier to guarantee at this size.

You always reach a founder

Email us and one of us answers — not a funnel, not a script. If we're a fit we'll tell you, and if we're not we'll tell you that too.

That's the whole story, honestly told: two founders, real data guarantees, and work we can stand behind. See exactly what we guarantee, or start a conversation.
