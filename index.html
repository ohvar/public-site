How it works

Sensitive in. Analysis-ready out. Nothing raw in between.

Three stages, one hard boundary. Identifiable data stays home; only de-identified data ever moves.

  1. 01

    On-site ingestion

    Your identifiable records are read and processed where they already live — on your hardware, inside your building, offline. Nothing raw is uploaded, mirrored, or cached off-premises.

  2. 02

    Enforced de-identification

    Identifiers are stripped and transformed before anything crosses the boundary. De-identification isn't a policy we ask you to trust — it's a gate the pipeline physically passes through.

  3. 03

    Analysis-ready data

    What comes out is clean, structured, and ready for AI. Cloud models only ever see de-identified data, so you get modern analytics without the exposure that usually comes with it.
